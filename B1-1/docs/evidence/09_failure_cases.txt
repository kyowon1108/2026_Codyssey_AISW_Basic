$ ps -eo pid,stat,comm,args | grep '[a]gent-app-linux'
 2999 Ts   bash            bash -c echo $$ > /tmp/zombie_parent.pid; /tmp/agent-app-linux-zombie 1 & kill -STOP $$
 3000 Z    agent <defunct> [agent-app-linux] <defunct>
(exit code: 0)
  -> pgrep 은 좀비를 '살아있는 프로세스'로 반환한다

$ pgrep -af agent-app-linux | grep -v 'bash -lc'
2999 bash -c echo $$ > /tmp/zombie_parent.pid; /tmp/agent-app-linux-zombie 1 & kill -STOP $$
3000 agent-app-linux
(exit code: 0)
  -> monitor.sh 는 상태 Z 를 걸러내고 실패로 판정해야 한다
